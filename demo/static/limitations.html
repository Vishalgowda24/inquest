Known Limitations & Boundaries

Explicit boundaries, verified failure modes, and operational risks before real incident deployment.

Known Failure

1. Silent Causes (Top-1 Miss Rate: 65.2%)

Across all 69 evaluated silent_cause cases, V3 top-1 accuracy is only 24/69 (34.8%), representing a 65.2% miss rate. When an incident occurs without emitting change events, deployment metadata, or direct alerting signals, score-margin fusion dilutes topological propagation signals. While top-3 recall reaches 94.2% (65/69), top-1 ranking cannot be trusted for unalerted, silent failures.

Algorithmic Regressions

2. Regressions on Seasonality & Coincident Events

Under score-margin fusion (V3), two scenario families suffered regressions relative to unweighted reciprocal rank fusion (V0):
- long_seasonal: Regressed from 69/69 (100%) to 57/69 (82.6%) top-1 hits (-12 cases). Diurnal variations create spurious score margins amplified by V3.
- coincident_restart: Regressed from 67/69 to 65/69 top-1 hits (-2 cases) due to non-causal restarts occasionally commanding high change scores.

Verification Caveat

3. S04 Verification Recall (15.4%) & Decoy Vulnerability

S04 verified only 40 out of 260 true causes during P7 evaluation (15.38% recall), and was repeatedly deceived by shared-ancestry decoys in shared_dependency and coincident_restart topologies. Consequently, automated S04 candidate re-ranking is disabled (verification_rerank: false), and verification check statuses are explicitly designated as experimental.

Evaluation Boundary

4. Numeric Probability Model Unvalidated

P10 evaluation revealed that parametric logistic regression clustered all 280 test probabilities into the [0.9, 1.0] bucket with no resolution on medium or low confidence events, yielding Brier and ECE scores no better than naive baselines. Numeric probabilities have been excised from default incident reports, replaced with rule-based assessment flags (standard, review_recommended, insufficient_evidence).

Baseline Nuance

5. Simulator First-Alert Timing Bias

In simulator scenarios without engineered alert delays or noise, the simulator triggers the causal alert first in 98.6% of cases (476/483). In clean environments, the naive alert_order baseline matches the ensemble's accuracy (98.6% vs 98.8%). Inquest's aggregate advantage is derived entirely from cases featuring delayed alerts, alert noise, or silent causes.

Data Boundary

6. Real Incident Data Status: Not Evaluated

Not evaluated on real data. The data/real/ directory is absent. All evidence, benchmarks, evaluations, and accuracy numbers originate strictly from deterministic synthetic microservice simulations. Real-world incident evaluation requires offline data adapters defined in docs/REAL_DATA.md.

Synthetic demo: evidence is from simulated incidents only.

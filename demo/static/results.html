Family-Mix Caveat: Ensemble vs Alert-Order Baseline

Across all 948 cases, the ensemble achieves 868 top-1 hits (91.6%) versus alert_order's 618 (65.2%), an aggregate difference of +250 cases (+26.4 percentage points).

However, this aggregate advantage is entirely dependent on scenario family mix:
On the 7 families where alerts fire predictably without corruption or delay (clean_bad_deploy, coincident_restart, missing_telemetry, partial_graph, red_herring_deploy, red_herring_hard, shared_dependency), alert_order achieves 476/483 top-1 hits (98.6%), essentially matching the ensemble's 477/483 (98.8%).

The ensemble's entire net lead arises from families with designed alert failure modes (e.g. late_alerting_cause +62, cause_alerts_2_4 +57, noisy_alerts +51, unordered_plausible_alerts +42, silent_cause +24). Inquest's value is in resilience when alert timing is deceptive or delayed.
